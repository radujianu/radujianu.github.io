CIS6930 – Special Topics - Visualization

 

Class description

In this class students will be familiarized with Information Visualization principles, techniques, and algorithms. They will implement a few fundamental visualization techniques. They will read and discuss seminal and state of the art research papers on the subject. Finally, students will experience the research pipeline in its entirety by participating in a simulated funding process and implementing a collaborative visualization project. Specifically, students will meet potential collaborators, in and outside the department, decide on projects that are likely to yield valuable research contributions, write short project proposals, review each other's proposals and decide collectively which proposals are worth “funding”, implement the project, write a short paper documenting the results, and present that paper orally.


Student Goals
To learn fundamental information visualization principles, techniques, and algorithms
To practice the basic programming elements that will let you turn raw data into visualizations
To become exposed to state of the art visualization ideas and principles
To learn to assess the value of research ideas, flesh them out into research proposals, and turn those into actual research contributions
To present work in writing and orally
To become exposed to the basics of the scientific funding process
To experience collaborative, potentially interdisciplinary, work

Prerequisites
Some programming experience, preferably Java
